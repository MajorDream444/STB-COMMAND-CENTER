# The 14-Point Business Bleed Map
**STB Canonical Doctrine · Version 2.0 · Upgraded from NotebookLM deep critique**
**Supersedes:** 12-Point Bleed Map (original)

---

## Why 14, Not 12

The original 12-point map maps the external machinery of a business beautifully — attention, conversion, follow-up, delivery. But for somatic practitioners, yoga teachers, spiritual educators, and relationship-driven visionaries, the most fatal bleeds are rarely a broken email sequence or a missing top-of-funnel.

The critical insight from the NotebookLM critique:

> "You can patch every single hole in the bucket, but if the person carrying the bucket collapses from exhaustion, the water still spills."

A practitioner may have flawless attention metrics, excellent conversion rates, and beautifully organized delivery systems — and still be hemorrhaging because they are:
- Chronically undercharging for the immense emotional labor they provide
- Operating at an unsustainable energetic deficit to fulfill the service
- Running a perfectly engineered funnel that is actively destroying the founder

The 12-point map diagnosed the business entity. The 14-point map also diagnoses the systemic health of the host carrying it.

## The Two New Points: Full Specification

### Point 13 — Financial Alignment

**The question:** "Are you fully booked but still struggling financially?"

**What we're checking:**
- Is the pricing model aligned with the actual emotional and energetic cost of delivery?
- Are they charging for their time, or for their transformation?
- Are they undercharging as a trauma response ("I don't want to price people out") rather than a business decision?

**Why it matters for this demographic:**
Somatic practitioners and healers frequently price their services at commodity rates while delivering deeply bespoke, emotionally intensive work. The bleed isn't in the funnel — it's in the margin. You can fill every seat and still run a business that is destroying the founder financially.

**Diagnostic question to ask:**
*"If you doubled your rate right now, how many clients would you lose — and would you actually make more money overall?"*

**Sprint mapping:**
Financial Alignment Bleed → Pricing Architecture Sprint

---

### Point 14 — Founder Capacity

**The question:** "Can you sustain this delivery at current volume?"

**What we're checking:**
- Is the founder operating at a chronic energetic deficit to fulfill the service?
- Is the delivery model designed for the founder's actual capacity, or someone else's?
- Are they saying yes to every client because they can't imagine saying no?

**Why it matters for this demographic:**
For relationship-driven visionaries, their methodology is linked to their personal energy. When that energy is depleted, the quality of the work collapses — and the business collapses with it. Unlike a SaaS product that runs whether the founder is rested or not, a healer's business IS the healer.

**Diagnostic question to ask:**
*"On a scale of 1–10, how would you rate your energy at the end of a full client week — and is that sustainable for the next 12 months?"*

**Sprint mapping:**
Founder Capacity Bleed → Capacity Architecture Sprint (restructure delivery model, group format, recorded assets)

## Maintaining Speed: The 15–20 Minute Protocol

Adding Points 13 and 14 does NOT bloat the diagnostic into a 90-minute therapy session. The key is sequencing:

**Minutes 0–10:** Run Points 1–12 (acquisition and operations scan)
**Minutes 10–15:** Run Points 13–14 ONLY if the founder reports a structural puzzle — "I'm busy but not profitable" or "I'm exhausted all the time"

Points 13 and 14 are conditional gates, not mandatory stops. They activate when the symptom pattern suggests the leak is inside the founder rather than inside the business machinery.

The machine-readable evidence layer tracks:
- [ ] A pricing review was completed (documented minimum viable rate)
- [ ] A capacity ceiling was defined (maximum sessions per week)
- [ ] At least one "no" was recorded in the sprint window
