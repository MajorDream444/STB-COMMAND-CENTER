# The Stability Seeker Archetype
**STB Canonical Doctrine · Layer 3 Reference · Stage 01 Input**

---

## Who This Person Is

The Stability Seeker is the primary client of the Spark Lab STB system.

They are not a beginner entrepreneur. They are not someone who lacks ideas, talent, or work ethic. They are someone who has been doing the work — sometimes for years — and still feels like the ground under their business is not solid.

They are gifted. They are often overqualified for the work they're doing. They are frequently sought out by people who need what they offer. And they are quietly exhausted by the gap between how much they give and how stable their business actually is.

---

## The Core Wound

The Stability Seeker's primary pain is not failure. It is **invisible leakage**.

They are not failing in obvious ways. They have clients. They have income. They have a reputation in their community. But something is always slipping through the cracks:
- Leads who were interested and then went quiet
- Content that performed well but didn't convert
- Clients who loved the work but never referred anyone
- Time spent on delivery that leaves nothing for growth
- A sense that if they stopped working, everything would stop with them

The business is technically alive. But it doesn't feel stable. It feels like running.

---

## Who They Are (Demographics)

**Primary profiles:**
- Somatic practitioners, breathwork facilitators, nervous system coaches
- Yoga teachers, movement educators, embodiment practitioners
- Spiritual educators, intuitive coaches, energy healers
- Business coaches and consultants who built their practice through relationships
- Retreat facilitators, workshop leaders, group program creators
- Creatives and artists who have turned their practice into a business

**Typical characteristics:**
- 3–12 years into their practice or business
- Revenue between $2,000–$15,000/month — often inconsistent
- Primarily serve clients one-to-one or in small groups
- Built their audience through organic relationship, not advertising
- Based in or connected to wellness communities, often globally dispersed
- Frequently located in Bali, Southeast Asia, UK, Australia, Canada, USA

**What they have already tried:**
- Multiple online courses about business, marketing, or systems
- Various coaching programs that focused on mindset over mechanics
- Attempts at content consistency that eventually collapsed
- Hiring VAs or assistants who needed more management than they saved
- Funnel builders, CRM tools, and automation platforms they never fully implemented

---

## The Psychological Profile

**They think in relationships, not transactions.**
Every business decision is filtered through "how will this feel to the person receiving it?" This is a strength — it's why their clients trust them deeply. It becomes a liability when it prevents them from building systems that feel impersonal.

**They have a complex relationship with money.**
Charging what their work is worth feels in direct tension with their values of accessibility and service. Many have absorbed beliefs that spiritual or healing work shouldn't be commodified. This produces chronic undercharging and a financial bleed that no amount of client work can fix.

**They are highly intuitive but poorly served by intuition alone.**
Their intuition is accurate — they sense what their clients need, what a collaboration will produce, what content will land. But intuition without evidence creates a business that feels like it's always guessing. They need systems that translate their intuition into repeatable action.

**They want empowerment, not complexity.**
They do not want to become technologists. They do not want to learn AI, understand agent orchestration, or build funnels. They want to give their gift and have the business part work. The moment an offer feels like homework, they disengage.

**They are in a double bind around visibility.**
Their work is deeply personal. Putting it online feels exposing. But staying invisible means the people who need them can't find them. Many Stability Seekers oscillate between over-sharing (bursts of content that feel authentic but don't convert) and going quiet (pulling back when the vulnerability cost gets too high).

---

## What They Are Not

**Not a startup founder.**
They are not optimizing for scale, funding, or exit. They are optimizing for sustainability — a business that supports their life without consuming it.

**Not someone who needs more information.**
They have read the books, taken the courses, listened to the podcasts. More information is not the solution. A specific, verified next action is.

**Not someone who will respond to hustle culture.**
Language like "crush it," "scale to six figures," or "build your empire" lands wrong. It signals that the framework doesn't understand them, which immediately closes the door.

**Not someone who needs to be told their work has value.**
They know their work has value. What they need is a system that reflects that value to the market clearly enough that the market can respond.

## The Stability Seeker's Language

These are the words and phrases they actually use when describing their situation:

**About the business:**
- "I feel like I'm always starting over."
- "I have clients but I never feel stable."
- "Everything lives in my head."
- "I get engagement but no bookings."
- "I know I should be more consistent but..."
- "I don't know what I'm actually selling."
- "I feel like I'm running two jobs — the work and the business."

**About themselves:**
- "I'm not a business person."
- "I don't want to be salesy."
- "I just want to do the work."
- "I know I need systems but I don't know where to start."
- "I've tried everything and nothing sticks."

**About technology and AI:**
- "I don't understand any of this."
- "I don't want to become a tech person."
- "I'm scared of getting it wrong."
- "Can this actually work for someone like me?"

---

## What Changes When the Bleed Stops

When a Stability Seeker's business is stable, they describe it differently:

- "I know what I'm offering and it's clear."
- "People find me and understand what I do without me having to explain it."
- "I have enough — not infinite — just enough to feel safe and do good work."
- "The business doesn't stop when I take a weekend."
- "I'm not exhausted by the business part anymore."

This is the target state. Not empire. Not six figures by Friday. Stability. Clarity. The feeling that the ground is solid.

## Application to the STB Command Center

**The Today screen** is designed around the Stability Seeker's daily experience. One focus. One action. No overwhelm. The system asks one thing of them per day and acknowledges when it gets done.

**The Evidence Log** speaks directly to the Stability Seeker's tendency to run on vibes. Binary proof — did the thing or didn't — replaces the emotional weather that typically governs their sense of momentum.

**The Offer Clarity screen** addresses the most common Stability Seeker presenting problem: unclear or untranslated offer language. The AI Offer Architect uses the worldview extraction method to translate their instinctive language into a First Sentence that converts.

**Onboarding questions** are calibrated to the Stability Seeker's vocabulary:
- "What do you do?" (not "Describe your value proposition")
- "Where is the bleed?" (operator language only — externally: "What's the thing that keeps coming back unsolved?")
- "What does stable look like?" (not "What are your revenue goals?")

**AI suggestions** are framed as a thinking partner, not an authority. "Claude suggests:" followed by a confirm/dismiss — the Stability Seeker always makes the final call. The system never acts without their consent.
